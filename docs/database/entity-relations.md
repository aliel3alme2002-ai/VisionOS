# Entity Relations

## Overview

VisionOS utilizes a normalized relational model. The hierarchy flows downwards from the Tenant to operational assets and transient data.

## Hierarchical Relationships

### Top Level
- `Tenant` is the root entity.
- A `Tenant` has many `Users`.
- A `Tenant` has many `Sites`.
- A `Tenant` has many `Licenses`.

### Location Hierarchy
- A `Site` belongs to a `Tenant`.
- A `Site` has many `Buildings`.
- A `Building` belongs to a `Site`.
- A `Building` has many `Floors`.
- A `Floor` belongs to a `Building`.
- A `Floor` has many `Zones`.

### Hardware & Assets
- An `EdgeBox` belongs to a `Site`.
- A `Camera` belongs to a `Zone`.
- A `Device` belongs to a `Zone`.

### Rules & Alerts
- A `Rule` belongs to a `Tenant` and optionally targets a `Site` or `Zone`.
- An `Event` is generated by a `Camera`, `Device`, or `EdgeBox`.
- An `Alert` is triggered by a `Rule` reacting to an `Event`.
- A `Notification` is triggered by an `Alert` and sent to a `User`.

## Cardinality Examples

| Source Entity | Relationship | Target Entity | Cardinality | Nullable? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tenant | One-to-Many | Site | 1:N | No |
| Site | One-to-Many | Building | 1:N | No |
| Zone | One-to-Many | Camera | 1:N | No |
| Tenant | One-to-Many | User | 1:N | No |
| EdgeBox | One-to-Many | Event | 1:N | No |
